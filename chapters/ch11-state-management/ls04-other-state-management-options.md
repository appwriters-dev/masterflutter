While Provider and BLoC are the most popular state management solutions, there are many other options available. Some of them are:

- **Riverpod**: A complete rewrite of Provider that is more flexible and powerful.
- **MobX**: A state management library that makes it easy to connect your reactive data with the UI.
- **GetX**: A fast, stable, and lightweight state management solution.
- **Redux**: A predictable state container for Dart and Flutter apps.

## Projects

### 1. Research and Compare

Research the state management options listed above. Write a short summary of each one, and then compare them in a table. Include the following information in your comparison:

- Ease of use
- Learning curve
- Performance
- Community support

### 2. Choose a Solution

Based on your research, choose one of the state management solutions and re-create the counter app from the previous lessons using it.
